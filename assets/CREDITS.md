# Credits for files in assets/ and js/stardata.js

Everything else in Laniakea's Edge is generated in code. These are real data, used so the sky and the planets
are where and what they really are.

- **Earth surface** (`assets/sol/earth.jpg`): NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble, "land_shallow_topo_2048".
  Image by Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory. https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57752 . Public domain (NASA).
- **Earth clouds** (`assets/sol/clouds.jpg`): NASA Visible Earth, Blue Marble clouds, "cloud_combined_2048",
  resized to 1024 x 512. https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/57747 . Public domain (NASA).
- **Moon surface** (`assets/sol/moon.jpg`): NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit, LRO LROC colour
  map "lroc_color_poles_1k". https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720 . Public domain (NASA).
- **Stars** (`js/stardata.js`): HYG Database v4.1 by David Nash (astronexus),
  https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database . CC BY-SA 4.0; the derived subset is shared under the same license.
