Combat log

HardBurn

Six ships under your command, six coming the other way. Close the distance, strip their shields and break their line before they break yours.

Drag to orbit the view, scroll to zoom. Best with sound on; music and effects can be muted separately.

How to play

Each turn every one of your ships can move up to its movement allowance, fire each of its ready weapons once, and use its special ability if charged. Move and fire in any order. When you end your turn, the enemy does the same. Destroy all six enemy ships to win.

Giving orders

  • Click one of your ships to select it. Highlighted hexes show where it can move; click one to burn there.
  • Hover an enemy to see your odds. Click it to fire every ready weapon that can reach, or pick a single weapon first.
  • Shields absorb damage first and recharge each turn. Armor then blunts every hit to the hull.

Terrain

Large asteroids block movement and line of sight for direct-fire weapons, but they can be shot apart. Click one with a ship selected to fire on it; a shattered asteroid leaves a debris field behind. Debris fields cost double movement but give ships inside them cover. Destroyed ships break apart and leave a wreck field of the same kind. Frigates extend their point defense to nearby allies.
